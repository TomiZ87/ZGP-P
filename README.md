Zajic’s Ground Probe Program <b>(ZGP-P)</b> is a program about constructcting, programming, and developing stationary probes or embedded devices iteratively consisting of a microcontroller such as Arduino or Raspberry PI and other components, modules and gadgets. The devices' sensors will be measuring, collecting, processing,storing, and displaying the data in a user-friendly way. Each prototype will improve and/or add new features and have a set goal.

<h2>History</h2>
The idea of ZGP-P came in 2017 when I first encountered and used Arduino and temperature sensor together with my father and watching our country’s first mini probe skCUBE going to space. The probe’s success motivated us to learn more about microcontrollers and other components, so we made our first prototype and named it ZGP-01, and then I came with the idea of making videos about it. I made the video in my native language but abandoned the idea because of the school.
<br><br>
During the quarantine in 2020, I started programming in Unity and started making videos about it in English. I also had more time to watch something I like, one of them being space news - I have been watching almost every SpaceX and ESA - European Space Agency launch. I was impressed with the progress of the space industry in the resent years and their successes, so I decided to start again working on the probes/prototypes. I restarted the ZGP-P project and continued by remaking the ZGP-01 video. In the meanwhile the ZGP-02 was created and the program has got a new breath.
<br><br>
After ZGP-02, I focused again on my education. In 2025, I started maintaing repositories at a student organization at my university and decided to once again work on the programme - it has potential and it shows you how far you have advenced. In the beginning 2026, the repository has been slowly prepared for another rebirth of the program, but this time with proper documentation.

<h2>Goals</h2>
The goals of this program are to make the prototypes:
<ul>
  <li>Iteratively (+ improving on each iteration)</li>
  <li>Self-Sustainable*</li>
  <li>Durable</li>
  <li>Measuring, Collecting, Storing, and Displaying the telemetry</li>
</ul>

|* Self-Sustainable in a sense that they should be able to function for a predefined time perdiod (given by a energy source or set by @TomiZ87)

<h2>Testing</h2>
Every probe should be constructed for certain purposes, function, and test them. We will test every probe and make analyses to determine, where we can improve, advance, and add new features and functions.
